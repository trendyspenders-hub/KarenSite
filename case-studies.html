General Contractor Construction

From Failed Audit to Zero Deficiencies

The Challenge

A 50-employee construction company failed a prime contractor audit on documentation gaps – no site-specific fall protection plans and missing orientation records. Two GC clients threatened to remove them from bid lists.

The Solution

We rebuilt the safety program around their actual scopes of work, created site-specific templates supervisors could complete in minutes, and ran field training for all crew leads over three weeks.

The Result: Passed the re-audit with zero deficiencies and regained eligibility on two GC bid lists within 60 days.

Residential Care Facility Healthcare

Cutting Handling Injury Claims by More Than Half

The Challenge

A 60-staff care facility saw injury claims rise three years straight – 70% of them from patient handling. Staff morale and retention were suffering along with the claims ledger.

The Solution

We assessed lift equipment, wrote a safe handling program matched to resident mobility levels, and trained every care aide in staggered sessions that didn't disrupt coverage.

The Result: Handling injury claims dropped 55% in the first year, and the facility passed licensing review with no safety findings.
