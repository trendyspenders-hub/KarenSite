North Okanagan

Safety Programs Built for Vernon's Economy

Vernon's economy reflects its position at the top of the Okanagan Valley, where the valley's agricultural and tourism character meets the Interior's industrial base. Construction is steady as the city grows and renovates its established neighbourhoods. Manufacturing and light industry operate along the highway corridors, serving both the local market and the broader Interior. Agriculture on the surrounding benchlands includes dairy, beef, and forage production alongside the region's characteristic orchards and vineyards. And the tourism economy, centred on Kalamalka Lake and SilverStar Mountain Resort, employs a large seasonal workforce across hospitality, recreation, and services.

The North Okanagan's hazard profile blends the valley's agricultural and tourism exposures with the Interior's industrial character. Summer heat is a genuine occupational exposure for outdoor employers. The tourism sector's seasonal staffing patterns create orientation and supervision challenges that urban-only programs handle poorly. Construction employers face the same pre-qualification expectations as their Lower Mainland competitors. And manufacturing operations carry the standard industrial compliance stack with the added reality of a smaller local labour pool where cross-trained workers move between roles and hazards quickly.

Alcantara Safety Solutions serves employers across the North Okanagan from Vernon through Coldstream, Armstrong, and the surrounding district. Engagements run remote-first at the same fixed fees we quote everywhere in British Columbia, with site visits scheduled where the project genuinely calls for them. Every program is written from scratch against the Regulation and the specific mix of hazards your operation carries.
