What to Prepare Before Requesting a Safety Plan Quote

The fastest way to get an accurate, fixed-fee quote is to have a clear picture of your operation before the first conversation. None of this needs to be formal — rough numbers are fine — but the more you can share, the tighter the scope and the fewer surprises later.

1. What your crews actually do

List your main activities in plain language — for example, "commercial framing," "underground utility installation," "warehousing and order fulfilment." Scope is priced on the work, not the industry label, so describe tasks rather than SIC codes.

2. Where the work happens

One site or many? Fixed addresses or mobile crews? Work at client premises, heights, confined spaces, or near energized equipment all change which parts of the OHS Regulation your program must address.

3. How many people are involved

Your crew size drives whether you need a joint health and safety committee or a worker health and safety representative under the Workers Compensation Act, and how your orientation and training records must be structured.

4. What documents you already have

Existing safety manuals, site-specific plans, training certificates, and incident history — even outdated ones — tell us what can be kept, what must be rebuilt, and what your prime contractors or clients have already accepted. Digging through old binders is normal.

5. Equipment and higher-risk tasks

Make a rough list of the machinery and processes that worry you most: forklifts, excavations, silica exposure, fall hazards, lockout points. These are the sections regulators and client auditors read first.

6. Your deadline and why

A bid closing date, an audit, a COR submission, or a regulator order each changes what must be prioritized. A real deadline gets a real schedule.

What you do not need

You do not need to have any of this organized. Half of a coordinator's job is pulling the pieces together. If all you have today is a phone number and a description of the work, that is enough to start the conversation.
