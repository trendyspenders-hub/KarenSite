# Google Business Profile Checklist — Alcantara Safety Solutions

**Owner-facing checklist. Not started — nothing has been created, claimed, or edited.** Prepared 2026-10-21.

The Google Maps embed on the website stays removed, per your earlier instruction. A Google Business Profile (GBP) is a separate thing — a business listing in Google's local results — and this checklist is for whenever *you* decide to set one up. Do not create it until you are ready to answer every eligibility question below honestly.

---

## 1. Eligibility verification (decide BEFORE creating anything)

- [ ] **Does the business qualify?** GBP requires either (a) a location customers can visit, or (b) a **service-area business** (SAB) that goes to customers. A consultancy working at client sites qualifies as an SAB.
- [ ] **Service-area configuration:** as an SAB, you set the cities/areas served and **hide your home address** from the public. Decide now whether you are comfortable with Google knowing your address (it is required internally even when hidden publicly).
- [ ] **Name eligibility:** the profile name must be the real-world business name — "Alcantara Safety Solutions" — with no added keywords like "Safety Plans BC" (that is a suspension risk).

## 2. Business name and category

- [ ] Name: **Alcantara Safety Solutions** (exactly as used everywhere else).
- [ ] Primary category: choose the closest honest fit — candidates to evaluate in the live category picker: *Safety consultant*, *Business management consultant*, or *Occupational safety and health service*. Pick the one that best matches how customers search, not the most flattering one.
- [ ] Secondary categories: add only genuinely offered services (e.g., consulting categories). Never add unearned categories for reach.

## 3. Location / service area

- [ ] Hide street address (SAB setup).
- [ ] Add service areas as the **cities you genuinely serve** — the same set as the website's service-area pages. Do not list all of BC if realistic coverage is the Lower Mainland; overstated service areas are a suspension trigger.

## 4. Hours and contact details (must match the website exactly)

- [ ] Phone: (778) 984-1988 — identical format to the site.
- [ ] Website: https://asafetysolutions.com
- [ ] Hours: enter real working hours. If hours vary, leave them unset rather than guessing. The site's schema currently says Mo–Fr 08:00–18:00 — update whichever one is wrong so they match.
- [ ] Holiday hours: update when applicable; wrong holiday hours generate disproportionate negative reviews.

## 5. Services actually offered

- [ ] List only the three services the business actually sells: custom safety plan creation, compliance consulting, and risk assessments. (The industries served are a separate field.)
- [ ] One-line description per service, honest scope only — no guaranteed outcomes.

## 6. Photographs (only approved, real ones)

- [ ] Logo (already have: logo files on the site).
- [ ] Real work photos: site walk-throughs, document reviews, training sessions — only photos you own and that contain no client-identifiable details without written client permission.
- [ ] A real headshot of Karen for the profile — people hire consultants, not logos.
- [ ] No stock photos presented as the business. Google has been known to soft-penalize stock imagery on profiles, and it misleads searchers.

## 7. A process for requesting honest reviews (no incentives, no gating)

This is the part most businesses get wrong. The rules:

- **Never pay, discount, or gift for a review.** Incentivized reviews violate both Google's policy and Canada's Competition Act.
- **No review gating** — do not screen for happy customers before asking. Ask everyone you complete work for, or ask no one.
- **No self-reviews, no employee reviews, no family reviews.** Google detects these and removes them; repeated patterns suspend the profile.
- **Suggested process (paper-trail friendly):**
  1. When a project closes and the client expresses satisfaction — verbally or by email — reply: *"Glad it landed well. If you're open to it, an honest Google review helps other BC businesses find us: [your GBP review link]. No pressure either way."*
  2. Send once. Never follow up more than once, and never condition anything on the review.
  3. Respond to every review — positive and negative — within a few business days. Keep responses professional; never confirm a client relationship in the response itself (confidentiality).
- [ ] Create your review link (from the GBP dashboard: Ask for reviews) and save it somewhere handy.
- [ ] Set a calendar reminder to respond to new reviews weekly.

## 8. Verification practicalities

- [ ] Google will verify the business — typically by video, phone, or (for hidden-address SABs) video showing proof of the business. Have ready: business registration, the website open, signage/branding if any exists, proof you operate (vehicle, equipment, documents with the business name).
- [ ] Verification can take days to weeks. Do not create the profile until you can complete verification promptly — an unverified profile sitting stale can be harder to verify later.

## 9. Ongoing (monthly, 15 minutes)

- [ ] Check for new reviews; respond.
- [ ] Add a real photo or two when you have them.
- [ ] Post a short update if genuinely newsworthy (new service detail, regulatory change affecting clients) — GBP posts are optional, not a ranking lever worth heavy time.
- [ ] Confirm hours still accurate before long weekends.
