Fraser Valley

Safety Programs Built for Langley's Economy

Langley is two economies sharing one name. The Township's industrial districts – Campbell Heights, Gloucester, and the Highway 1 corridor – house one of the Lower Mainland's deepest concentrations of manufacturing and distribution operations, from building-products manufacturers and fabricators to national logistics hubs. The rural south and east carry a different character: one of Canada's premier equine communities, berry and greenhouse operations, and agri-industrial businesses that have supplied the region for generations. And over both hangs the same growth story transforming the Fraser Valley – the Surrey-Langley SkyTrain extension is coming, industrial land keeps absorbing, and construction is constant.

That dual character creates an unusual compliance landscape. A Gloucester distribution centre and a 40-acre equine facility face almost no overlapping hazards, yet both answer to the same WorkSafeBC OHS Regulation, the same orientation obligations, and the same inspection authority. What they need from a safety program is entirely different – which is exactly why template binders fail both of them.

Alcantara Safety Solutions works with Langley employers across this whole spectrum: manufacturers preparing for customer audits, distribution operators formalizing forklift and traffic programs, agricultural operations building their first real orientation system, and the construction firms raising the Township's next industrial parks. Every plan is written from scratch, on-site, at a fixed fee quoted before we begin.

Local Industries

Who We Serve in Langley

Manufacturing & Fabrication

Campbell Heights and Gloucester house manufacturers of every description – metal fabrication, building products, plastics, and specialty equipment among them. The compliance stack is well-defined but demanding: machine guarding and lockout, WHMIS 2015 chemical management, noise exposure programs, overhead crane and rigging documentation, and the customer vendor audits that increasingly arrive before WorkSafeBC does. We write manufacturing programs that satisfy both audiences – the prevention officer and the procurement department – because Langley manufacturers routinely answer to both.

Warehousing & Distribution

Langley's Highway 1 frontage has made it a natural logistics hub, and its distribution centres run the full modern warehouse hazard profile: powered industrial equipment fleets requiring documented training and competency verification, high-bay racking with inspection and load signage obligations, conveyor and sortation systems, pedestrian traffic management, and the relentless pressure of throughput targets. We build warehouse programs that supervisors can enforce on a busy floor – traffic plans people actually follow, inspection routines leads actually run, and documentation that survives a vendor audit.

Agriculture & Equine Operations

South Langley's agricultural belt is serious working countryside: berry and field crop operations, greenhouses and nurseries, and an equine community that anchors one of Canada's densest concentrations of horse facilities. Agricultural employers are fully covered by WorkSafeBC – machinery guarding, chemical storage and handling, seasonal worker orientation, and young worker supervision are all regulated obligations. Equine operations add their own profile: large animal handling, arena and facility maintenance, and riding instruction risk management. We write farm and facility programs in plain language, built for family-scale operations and commercial producers alike.

Construction & Trades

The Township's growth – industrial park expansion, residential development, and the coming SkyTrain corridor – keeps Langley's construction sector in constant motion. Contractors here face the standard compliance load: fall protection, excavation, prime contractor coordination, and the documentation demands of increasingly sophisticated procurement. We write site-specific plans and subcontractor packages sized for Langley's typical operator: lean, busy, and bidding work where safety documentation is now table stakes.

Agriculture & Greenhouse Operations

Langley's rural south and east carry one of the province's most productive farm belts – berry and greenhouse operations, nurseries, and the equine industry around them. Farm compliance is unforgiving: machinery guarding and entanglement hazards, confined spaces in silos and manure systems, chemical storage, and seasonal worker orientation, all under WorkSafeBC scrutiny. We build programs in plain language, sized for operations where the workforce changes with the season.

WorkSafeBC Compliance

What Langley Employers Need to Know

Langley employers work under the same province-wide OHS Regulation, but the Township's sector mix shapes what inspectors prioritize. In manufacturing and distribution, machine guarding, lockout, and powered equipment training lead the citation lists. On farms and nurseries, machinery entanglement, chemical storage, and undocumented seasonal orientations are the recurring failures. Across every Langley sector, the foundational gaps stay the same: no written program, no orientation records, no joint committee where one is required.

One Langley reality deserves emphasis: the Township is full of established family businesses – farms, shops, and fabricators in their second or third generation – where safety practice is excellent but safety documentation has never been formalized. WorkSafeBC does not grade on tradition; undocumented good practice fails an inspection just as completely as bad practice. Our work with Langley's established businesses is often less about changing how they operate and more about finally writing down what they already do, properly, in the format the Regulation demands.
