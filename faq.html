Services & Process

Working With Us

A safety plan coordinator designs, writes, and maintains the health and safety program your regulators and clients expect — written plans, hazard assessments, training records, inspection systems, and audit prep — so you stay compliant without hiring a full-time safety manager.

Every plan is written from scratch for your operation. Templates fail inspections because they describe a business you don't have; regulators and prime contractors can spot them immediately. Our plans reflect your actual sites, equipment, crew size, and jurisdictions.

Most single-site plans are delivered in two to four weeks depending on complexity and how quickly we receive your operational details. Rush delivery for pre-qualification or inspection deadlines is available — tell us your date and we'll work backwards from it.

It starts with a free consultation to understand your operation and requirements. You receive a fixed quote before any work begins. We then interview key staff, review your existing documentation, draft the plan, and refine it with you until it's right.
