Metro Vancouver

Safety Programs Built for Richmond's Economy

Richmond's economy runs on movement. Vancouver International Airport anchors an aviation and logistics cluster that operates around the clock; the Fraser River's south arm hosts port terminals and transload operations — including the half-kilometre warehouses where the province's forest products are containerized for export; and the city's food processing sector, from Steveston's seafood heritage to modern frozen food and packaging plants, feeds domestic and international markets alike. Richmond manufacturers produce everything from wood and paper products to clean energy systems, telecommunications equipment, and precision instruments — and they choose Richmond precisely because multi-modal access lets them import inputs and ship finished goods faster than almost anywhere else in Canada.

What ties these sectors together is a shared compliance profile: high-throughput operations where production pressure and hazard exposure rise together. Forklift traffic never stops in a cross-dock warehouse. Refrigeration systems run continuously in food plants. Seasonal surges bring waves of new and young workers into processing lines. Airport-adjacent work layers federally regulated environments next to provincially regulated ones. Richmond employers do not have the luxury of treating safety documentation as an afterthought — their customers, landlords, and insurers ask for it as routinely as WorkSafeBC does.

Alcantara Safety Solutions works with Richmond employers across this whole landscape: processors and packers, warehouse and distribution operators, manufacturers, aviation-adjacent contractors, and the construction firms building out the city's remaining industrial land. Every program is written from scratch, against the OHS Regulation parts that apply to your operation, at a fixed fee agreed before we start.

Local Industries

Who We Serve in Richmond

Food Processing & Seafood

Richmond is one of B.C.'s great food processing cities — seafood operations carrying Steveston's working harbour legacy forward, frozen food and ready-meal plants, bakeries, beverage producers, and the packaging manufacturers that support them. The hazard profile is well known to WorkSafeBC: ammonia refrigeration systems with their inspection and emergency procedure requirements, machine guarding and lockout on processing and packaging lines, cold storage work, sanitation chemicals, and the ergonomic toll of high-speed line work. We build food plant programs that align with your HACCP and food safety systems, so supervisors run one coherent set of procedures instead of two conflicting binders.

Warehousing, Distribution & Port Logistics

Between YVR's air cargo ecosystem, the Fraser River terminals, and the transload facilities that containerize B.C.'s forest products for export, Richmond's logistics sector never sleeps. Its compliance fundamentals are relentless: powered industrial equipment training and documented competency, rack inspection and load limits, pedestrian segregation in facilities where forklifts and order pickers share aisles, dock and trailer safety, and fatigue management across extended shifts. Vendor audits from national retailers and 3PLs add a second enforcement layer on top of WorkSafeBC. We write warehouse programs built to satisfy both.

Manufacturing

Richmond's manufacturing bench is unusually diverse — traditional wood and paper operations alongside clean energy systems, telecom equipment, green building materials, and precision instruments. The compliance stack shifts with the product: guarding and lockout for fabrication, WHMIS 2015 and exposure assessments for coating and chemical processes, noise programs for heavy production, and increasingly, the documentation that international customers and innovation partners expect from their supply chain. We map the Regulation to your actual process and document precisely what applies.

Aviation & Airport-Adjacent Operations

YVR is the region's gateway, and the businesses that serve it — ground handling, catering, cargo, construction, and maintenance contractors — operate in a distinctive regulatory neighbourhood. Provincial OHS obligations apply to most employer activities, but airport work layers on security-controlled environments, airside driving rules, and customer-mandated safety requirements from airlines and the airport authority. We help aviation-adjacent contractors build programs that satisfy WorkSafeBC, the airport's contractor requirements, and their airline customers simultaneously.

Agriculture & Nursery Operations

East Richmond's Agricultural Land Reserve holdings keep working farms inside the city limits — berry fields, nurseries, greenhouse operations, and the seasonal workforce that comes with them. Farm employers are fully covered by WorkSafeBC: machinery guarding, chemical handling and storage, seasonal worker orientation, and young worker supervision are all regulated obligations. We write farm safety programs in plain language, designed for operations where much of the workforce arrives with the harvest.

WorkSafeBC Compliance

What Richmond Employers Need to Know

Richmond's sector mix puts it squarely in WorkSafeBC's highest-attention territory. Food processing and warehousing are among the most inspected industries in the province, and the region's prevention officers know the recurring failure points cold: ammonia systems without current inspections, guards removed for changeovers and never replaced, forklifts operated by workers whose training documentation expired, and new-worker orientations that cannot be produced on request. The citations are predictable — which means they are preventable with documentation that is built and maintained properly.

One Richmond-specific pattern deserves mention: seasonal scaling. Processing and logistics operations here routinely double headcount for harvest, holiday, or contract surges — and every one of those workers triggers new and young worker orientation obligations from their first shift. Employers who orient properly in July and improvise in October are exactly who inspectors find. We build orientation systems designed for surge hiring: fast to deliver, consistent every time, and documented to a standard that survives both an inspection and a claim review.
