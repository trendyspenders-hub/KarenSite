Our Practice

Karen Corbet, Founder

With over five years of experience in the construction industry, Karen Corbet brings a multidisciplinary approach to workplace safety, organizational leadership, and human resources.

Having worked in senior leadership roles overseeing operations, people and culture, and occupational health and safety, Karen understands that successful businesses are built on more than policies and procedures. They are built on strong relationships, accountability, integrity, and systems that work in practice.

Her approach is rooted in the belief that people, culture, and operations are interconnected. By combining practical industry knowledge with a holistic understanding of organizational dynamics, Karen works to develop safety solutions that strengthen businesses from within.

Through Alcantara Safety Solutions, she is committed to helping organizations move beyond compliance, creating practical, sustainable systems that support their people, strengthen their operations, and make safety an integral part of everyday business.
