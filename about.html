Founder & Principal Coordinator

Karen Alcantara

For more than a decade, Karen has helped businesses develop safety documentation that not only meets compliance standards but actually creates safer workplaces. Her approach combines practical industry knowledge with a deep understanding of occupational health and safety requirements — every plan is built around the way your company actually operates, so the documentation is useful, compliant, and easy to implement.

A certified safety plan coordinator, she has built and audited programs across construction, manufacturing, warehousing, healthcare, food service, oil & gas, and logistics — 500+ custom safety plans over 12+ years — and she still walks every client site herself.

Whether you're a small contractor or a growing organization, her goal is simple: help you protect your people while giving you confidence during inspections, audits, and project bids.
