Safety Compliance Consulting

Find your compliance gaps and fix them — before an inspector, insurer, or incident finds them for you.

Contact Us Contact Us
Deliverables
Quick answer: A compliance consulting engagement costs between $1,500 and $5,000 and includes a regulation-by-regulation gap analysis, a documentation audit, a mock inspection, and a prioritized corrective action plan — so you know exactly where you stand before an inspector does.

What You Get

  • Regulation-by-regulation gap analysis against every WorkSafeBC or applicable provincial OHS standard.
  • Prioritized corrective action plan ranked by risk and citation likelihood, with realistic deadlines.
  • Documentation audit of your written programs, training records, and inspection logs.
  • Mock inspection so your team knows exactly what to expect when the real one comes.
  • Citation response support if you've already been written up and need to document abatement.
  • Plain-language executive summary for owners and leadership — no legal jargon.
