Workplace Risk Assessments

Find every hazard, score it, and know exactly what to fix first — before it hurts someone.

Contact Us Contact Us
Deliverables
Quick answer: A workplace risk assessment costs between $1,200 and $4,000, takes one to two days on site, and delivers a scored risk register with photo-documented findings and control recommendations ranked by priority — telling you exactly what to fix first.

What You Get

  • Full on-site hazard identification across equipment, processes, materials, and work environment.
  • Scored risk register — every hazard rated by severity and likelihood, in a working spreadsheet.
  • Photo-documented findings so there's no ambiguity about what needs attention.
  • Worker interviews that surface the near-misses and workarounds paperwork never shows.
  • Practical control recommendations following the hierarchy of controls — elimination first, PPE last.
  • Management briefing walking leadership through the top risks and recommended next steps.

Process

How an Assessment Runs

1

Pre-Visit Review

We study your incident history, existing plans, and industry hazard profile.

2

Site Walk & Interviews

One to two days on site observing real work and talking with your crew.

3

Scoring & Analysis

Hazards are scored, prioritized, and matched to practical controls.

4

Report & Briefing

You get the register, the photos, and a leadership walkthrough.
