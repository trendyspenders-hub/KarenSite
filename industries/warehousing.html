Warehousing & Distribution Safety Plans

Safety programs for high-throughput facilities where forklifts, racking, and speed pressure collide.

Get a Warehousing Safety Assessment Request a Quote
Know the Hazards
Quick answer: The top warehouse hazards are forklift-pedestrian incidents, damaged racking, repetitive strain injuries, and loading dock falls. Effective programs combine traffic management plans, operator certification (OSHA 1910.178), racking inspection routines, and rapid onboarding for seasonal staff.

Risks in Warehousing

Forklift Incidents

Pedestrian strikes and tip-overs are the top severe-injury source in distribution environments.

Racking & Falling Loads

Damaged uprights, overloading, and poor stacking put everyone below at risk.

Repetitive Strain

Pick rates and manual handling drive musculoskeletal claims — the costliest injury category.

Slips, Trips & Dock Hazards

Loading docks, trailer gaps, and congested aisles create constant fall exposure.

Speed Pressure

Quota-driven work encourages shortcuts — programs must work at operational tempo.

Temp & Seasonal Labor

High-churn workforces need fast, documented onboarding that actually sticks.

The Rulebook

Regulatory Requirements

Warehouse programs must address the standards regulators focus on in distribution:

  • OSHA 1910.178 Powered Industrial Trucks
  • Racking inspection & load capacity documentation
  • Emergency action & fire prevention plans
  • WorkSafeBC OHS Regulation Parts 4, 8 & 16 (BC)
  • New & young worker orientation requirements

How We Help

  • Forklift program builds Operator certification, traffic plans, and pedestrian separation that works at scale.
  • Racking inspection systems Scheduled inspections with damage-reporting workflows your team will use.
  • Rapid onboarding design Orientation packages that get seasonal workers safe and productive fast.
Case Study Warehousing
