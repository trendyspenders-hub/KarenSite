Commercial Office Safety Plans

Health and safety programs for offices, agencies, and professional workplaces — yes, the rules apply to you too.

Get an Office Safety Assessment Request a Quote
Know the Hazards
Quick answer: Office employers must still meet health and safety obligations: in BC, workplaces with 20+ workers need a joint health and safety committee and a formal program; everywhere, employers need emergency evacuation plans, violence-prevention policies, ergonomic risk management, and documented worker orientation. Low hazard doesn't mean no obligation — and remote/hybrid work has added new duties most offices haven't documented.

Risks in Commercial Offices

Ergonomic Strain

Workstation setup, repetitive strain, and sit-stand issues — the leading office claim category.

Fire & Emergency

Evacuation plans, floor wardens, and drills — fire code obligations most offices haven't documented.

Workplace Violence

Reception areas, terminations, and public access — violence-prevention policies are legally required.

Remote & Hybrid Work

Home-office ergonomics and check-in duties followed your staff home — policies need to catch up.

Slips & Trips

Cables, wet lobbies, and kitchen spills — mundane, common, and entirely preventable with procedures.

Committee Gaps

Joint health and safety committees required at 20+ workers (BC) — often missing or undocumented.

The Rulebook

Regulatory Requirements

Your program needs to address the standards inspectors actually cite in this sector:

  • WorkSafeBC joint committee requirements (20+ workers, BC)
  • Provincial & state violence-prevention program requirements
  • Fire code emergency planning obligations
  • Ergonomic / MSI prevention requirements
  • Annual program review & worker orientation duties

How We Help

  • Committee setup & training We establish your joint committee, train members, and build the meeting/minutes documentation WorkSafeBC expects.
  • Emergency planning Evacuation procedures, warden assignments, and drill documentation tailored to your floor plan.
  • Hybrid-work policies Remote-work ergonomics checklists and working-alone policies that cover your distributed team.
