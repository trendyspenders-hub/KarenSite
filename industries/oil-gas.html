Oil & Gas Safety Plans

Safety programs for producers, service companies, and energy contractors — built for high-hazard work and tough prequalification.

Know the Hazards
Quick answer: Oil and gas safety programs must cover H2S and hydrocarbon exposure, confined space entry, hot work, ground disturbance, and contractor management — plus the documentation demanded by prequalification platforms like ComplyWorks, Avetta, and ISNetworld. Energy Safety Canada COR certification is often a condition of bidding on producer contracts.

Risks in Oil & Gas

Fire & Explosion

Flammable hydrocarbons, hot work, and ignition sources — the defining hazard of the sector.

H2S & Toxic Gases

Hydrogen sulfide exposure in drilling, production, and confined spaces — detection and rescue planning are mandatory.

Confined Spaces

Tanks, vessels, and vaults with entry permits, atmospheric testing, and standby attendant requirements.

High-Pressure Systems

Wellheads, pipelines, and pressure equipment where failures are catastrophic.

Driving & Remote Sites

Long distances on lease roads and highways — driving is the sector's biggest killer.

Contractor Exposure

Multiple employers on one site — prime contractors answer for everyone under their coordination.

The Rulebook

Regulatory Requirements

Your program needs to address the standards inspectors actually cite in this sector:

  • Energy Safety Canada COR / SECOR certification
  • OSHA 29 CFR 1910.119 Process Safety Management (US)
  • WorkSafeBC OHS Regulation Parts 8, 9 & 23 (BC)
  • ComplyWorks, Avetta & ISNetworld prequalification
  • Ground disturbance & pipeline crossing agreements
