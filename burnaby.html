Safety Programs Built for Burnaby's Economy

Burnaby has anchored the Lower Mainland's industrial base for decades. Big Bend — the cluster of heavier industrial operations along Marine Way, Riverway, and Glenlyon — remains the densest legacy industrial submarket in the region, while Lake City and the Still Creek corridor have evolved toward tech-adjacent and light industrial occupiers: biopharma, electronics, and specialty manufacturers of every size. Layer on the high-rise construction reshaping Brentwood, Metrotown, and Edmonds, and Burnaby presents an unusually wide spread of workplace hazard profiles inside one municipal boundary.

That spread matters for compliance, because a Burnaby employer can face very different WorkSafeBC obligations depending on which side of the city it operates on. A specialty manufacturer in Big Bend answers to machine guarding, lockout, overhead cranes, and noise requirements. A contract electronics manufacturer in Still Creek answers to ergonomic and chemical handling requirements. A forming contractor on a Brentwood tower answers to fall protection, prime contractor coordination, and silica. Generic safety binders fail all three — just in different places.

Alcantara Safety Solutions builds safety programs for the full range of Burnaby operations. We are in the city regularly, walking production floors and construction sites alike, and every plan we write starts from what your people actually do — the equipment on your floor, the chemicals in your inventory, the subcontractors on your site — written against the specific parts of the OHS Regulation that apply to your industry.

Local Industries

Who We Serve in Burnaby

Big Bend Manufacturing

Big Bend remains one of the region's densest industrial districts, and its manufacturers carry some of the most inspection-prone hazard profiles in B.C.: machine guarding on presses and conveyors, lockout for maintenance and changeovers, overhead crane and rigging documentation, noise exposure programs, and WHMIS 2015 chemical management. WorkSafeBC prevention officers know this sector well and inspect it accordingly. We build manufacturing safety programs that satisfy both the prevention officer and the customer vendor audits that increasingly arrive first — one coherent set of procedures your supervisors can actually run.

Light Industrial & Specialty Manufacturing

The Still Creek and Lake City corridors house electronics assemblers, fabricators, biopharma labs, printers, and specialty manufacturers — operations where the hazards are quieter but the obligations are identical. Chemical inventories require WHMIS 2015 documentation and exposure assessments. Repetitive assembly work requires ergonomic attention. Soldering, coating, and cleaning processes can trigger exposure control plan requirements. We help Burnaby manufacturers identify which parts of the Regulation actually apply to their process, then document exactly that — no more, no less.

High-Rise Construction

Brentwood and Metrotown are two of the most active high-rise nodes in Western Canada, and the trades working those towers face the full weight of the Regulation: fall protection at complex leading edges, tower crane coordination, concrete formwork requirements, silica exposure control, hoist and elevator safety, and multi-employer coordination involving dozens of subcontractors. Whether you are a forming, glazing, electrical, or mechanical subcontractor, we write the site-specific plans and orientation packages that get you onto tier-one sites — and keep you there through the audits.

Warehousing & Distribution

Burnaby's central location and Highway 1 access make it a natural distribution hub, and its warehouse inventory ranges from legacy multi-tenant bays to modern high-clear facilities. The compliance fundamentals are consistent across all of them: powered industrial equipment training and documentation, rack inspections and load signage, pedestrian traffic management, dock safety, and injury-collision reporting discipline. We write warehouse programs that survive the scrutiny of the national retailers and 3PLs whose vendor audits increasingly drive this sector's documentation standards.

Film & Studio Support

Burnaby's studio infrastructure makes it a production hub in its own right, and the contractors who build, wire, rig, paint, and service sets face a distinctive compliance environment: compressed schedules, temporary structures, night work, and crews assembled quickly for short engagements. We help studio-adjacent trades maintain the orientations, hazard assessments, and supervision documentation that let them move between productions without their paperwork falling a contract behind.

WorkSafeBC Compliance

What Burnaby Employers Need to Know

Burnaby employers fall squarely inside WorkSafeBC's most active inspection territory, and the sector mix here shapes what officers look for. In manufacturing, machine guarding and lockout draw the most attention — and the most orders. On construction sites, fall protection and prime contractor documentation lead the list. Across every sector, the foundational citations stay stubbornly the same: no written program, no documented orientations, no joint committee where one is required, no exposure control plan where an assessment says one is needed.

One Burnaby-specific note: the city's industrial buildings skew older, and older buildings mean asbestos. Any renovation, demolition, or maintenance work in pre-1990 construction — which describes a large share of Big Bend and Still Creek inventory — triggers asbestos assessment obligations before work begins. Employers who discover this requirement after an inspector does have an expensive problem. We build qualified asbestos assessment and safe-work procedures into every program we write for Burnaby's legacy building stock.
