Landscaping Safety Plans

Safety programs for landscaping, lawn care, and grounds maintenance crews — seasonal, mobile, and equipment-heavy.

Get a Landscaping Safety Assessment Request a Quote
Know the Hazards
Quick answer: Landscaping safety programs must cover power equipment operation (mowers, trimmers, chainsaws, chippers), pesticide and fertilizer handling under WHMIS/HazCom and worker protection standards, heat stress prevention, and trailer/towing safety. High seasonal turnover means orientation and training documentation has to work fast — crews hired in April need to be documented by May.

Risks in Landscaping

Power Equipment

Mowers, trimmers, chainsaws, and wood chippers — projectile, contact, and entanglement hazards daily.

Heat Stress

Full-day outdoor work through summer — hydration, rest cycles, and acclimatization procedures prevent collapses.

Pesticides & Fertilizers

Chemical handling, mixing, and application with licensing, WHMIS/HazCom, and PPE requirements.

Noise Exposure

Extended equipment operation regularly exceeds 85 dB — hearing protection programs are required, not optional.

Towing & Road Safety

Loaded trailers between job sites — securement, lighting, and driver checks every trip.

Seasonal Turnover

New crews every spring — orientation and training records must keep pace with hiring.

The Rulebook

Regulatory Requirements

Your program needs to address the standards inspectors actually cite in this sector:

  • WHMIS 2015 / OSHA HazCom for chemical handling
  • EPA Worker Protection Standard / provincial pesticide rules
  • WorkSafeBC OHS Regulation & OSHA 1928 (agriculture-adjacent)
  • Hearing conservation requirements (85 dB+ exposure)
  • Commercial vehicle and trailer safety requirements

How We Help

  • Crew-ready procedures Equipment-specific safe-work procedures with photos — built for tailgate training, not binders.
  • Seasonal onboarding packages Orientation checklists and training records designed to document a new hire in their first week.
  • Chemical safety systems SDS binders, mixing/application procedures, and PPE requirements for your product list.
Case Study Landscaping

A landscaping company with 15 seasonal crews had zero documentation…

Every spring meant 40+ new hires with no recorded orientation — a serious exposure when a client-property incident triggered a WorkSafeBC review. We built a spring onboarding system: day-one orientation checklists, equipment sign-offs, and tailgate meeting templates for the whole season.

Result: Full training documentation for every seasonal worker, zero lost-time claims the following season, and lower WCB premiums at reassessment.
