How Ready Are You for an Inspection?

Answer 10 quick questions about your current safety program. You'll get an instant readiness score — plus a prioritized list of what to fix before an inspector finds it.

Question 1 of 10

1. Do you have a written health & safety policy, signed by senior management and reviewed within the last year?
2. Are written safe work procedures in place for your high-risk tasks (e.g., lockout, confined space, working at heights)?
3. Has a formal hazard assessment been completed for your tasks, equipment, and worksites?
4. Does every new worker complete a documented orientation before starting work?
5. Do you keep training records showing who was trained on what, and when?
6. Are regular workplace inspections performed and documented?
7. Is there a written emergency response plan (evacuation, first aid, emergency contacts) that workers know?
8. Do you have an incident investigation procedure — including how to report serious incidents immediately?
9. Is PPE specified by task, provided to workers, and covered in training?
10. Has your safety program been formally reviewed within the last 12 months?
