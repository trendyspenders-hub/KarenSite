How Your Information Is Handled

Your site photographs, worker names, and safety records stay private. In practice, that means:

  1. Used only for your project

    Site photos, employee details, and workplace records you share are used solely to build or assess your program — never for marketing, portfolios, or anyone else's work.

  2. Never published without written consent

    Nothing identifiable — no client names, logos, site images, or worker information — appears on this website or in any promotional material without your prior written approval.

  3. Shared only as needed, under NDA on request

    Your documents are not shared with third parties except as required to deliver the agreed work (for example, a certifying partner during COR preparation). A signed non-disclosure agreement is available for any engagement — just ask.

  4. Case studies are anonymized

    The results on our case studies page are real but de-identified: no client names, locations, or distinguishing details.

  5. You can ask for deletion

    Project files are kept only as long as needed for the work and applicable record-keeping, and you can request deletion of your materials at any time.

The fuller version lives in our Privacy Policy. If your organization has its own confidentiality or site-access requirements, tell us — we accommodate them routinely.
